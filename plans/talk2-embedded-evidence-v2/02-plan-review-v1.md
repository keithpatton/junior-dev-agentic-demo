# Talk 2 embedded-evidence revision — plan review v1

**Verdict:** proceed within the recorded human decisions.

## Finding 1 — evidence slides could become unreadable document screenshots

**Criterion:** a junior developer at the back of the room must identify the question, consequence, or decision without reading a full Markdown page.

**Resolution:** compose short, slide-native excerpts with one highlighted passage and a small path; do not reproduce whole files.

## Finding 2 — multiple overlapping loops could remain

**Criterion:** the audience should be able to repeat one workflow after the talk.

**Resolution:** make the eight verbs canonical and describe shape, authorise, and evaluate only as human responsibilities within that lifecycle.

## Finding 3 — the two-axis model could imply instant architectural competence

**Criterion:** greater reach must not be represented as transferred experience or diminished need for specialists.

**Resolution:** show both axes as fields of attention the developer must engage, while the speaker explicitly retains specialist judgement and accountability.

## Finding 4 — the app reveal could displace the closing lesson

**Criterion:** the talk must close on developer judgement, not a product demo.

**Resolution:** make the app a brief penultimate reveal; the final slide contains the provocations and questions.
