# Product

Apply/Forward helps a junior developer with 5–20 live job applications decide what to do in the next 30 minutes. The board records the search; the product outcome is momentum through one explained recommendation.

The user should be able to open the app, see a concrete next action with its opportunity and reason, then change a stage and understand why the recommendation changed. Recording, staging, deleting, and downloading applications are supporting capabilities.

The demo is local and single-user. Accounts, cloud data, reminders, scraping, team collaboration, and exhaustive scoring are out of scope.
