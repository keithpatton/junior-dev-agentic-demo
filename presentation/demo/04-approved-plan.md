# 04 — Revised plan and human approval

## Revision accepted

- recommend one next action using transparent deterministic rules;
- show the reason and a graceful fallback when data is incomplete;
- keep the initial product local and single-user;
- preserve application management and reload behaviour;
- treat persistence and download as separate system boundaries;
- make no privacy claim about notes until a later invariant and test exist;
- prove behaviour in both focused tests and the browser.

## Human decision

**Approved to implement.**

Conditions:

- no accounts, cloud services, automatic messages, or predictive scoring;
- stop and re-plan if implementation requires a new product or architecture decision;
- do not describe notes as private until the boundary has executable and browser evidence.

Approval is the moment the human accepts responsibility for what the agent is about to optimise.
