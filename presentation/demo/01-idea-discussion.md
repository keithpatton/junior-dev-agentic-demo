# 01 — Idea and interrogation

## Starting request

> Build a job application tracker for junior developers.

That sentence was useful enough to begin a conversation and too incomplete to authorise implementation.

## Questions that could change the result

- Who is struggling, and at what moment?
- Is recording applications the outcome, or merely the mechanism?
- What decision should become easier tomorrow morning?
- Which information might be sensitive?
- What would make a recommendation trustworthy rather than authoritative-looking?
- What is the smallest result we can prove locally?

## Decisions from the discussion

- The user is one early-career developer managing several active applications.
- The difficult moment is deciding where to spend the next 30 minutes.
- “Useful” means one recommended next action with a visible reason.
- Deterministic rules are preferable to opaque scoring for the first version.
- Accounts, collaboration, cloud sync, and automatic outreach are outside the first boundary.
- Free-text notes are potentially sensitive and need an explicit boundary before any privacy promise is made.

## Reframed outcome

> Help me decide what to do next across my active applications.

The idea changed before the code did.
