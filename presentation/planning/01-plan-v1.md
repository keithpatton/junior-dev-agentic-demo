# Plan v1: Beyond the Prompt

## Goal

Deliver a simple, hard-hitting 25:30 talk plus 4:30 Q&A that fulfils the published promise through two agentic engineering loops and three prepared product states.

## Non-goals

- teaching a coding-agent interface;
- teaching the complete Task Automation Framework;
- live generation;
- explaining long-running autonomous agents or graph engineering;
- arguing that code, algorithms, or technical knowledge no longer matter.

## Three audience nuggets

1. **The first working app is the least trustworthy moment.** Fluency and polish create confidence before product intent, boundaries, and evidence are understood.
2. **Use two loops.** Planning: interrogate → plan → challenge → approve. Implementation: build → challenge → prove → remember.
3. **Become an agentic builder.** Agents make product growth cheaper; the engineer gains earlier leverage across product, architecture, systems, operations, and evidence.

## Demonstration contract

- Primary demo is switching between App A, App B, and App C browser tabs.
- Prompts and Markdown artifacts are shown as prepared evidence.
- Opening source code is optional and limited to one system seam.
- Running the prepared privacy test is optional and time-boxed to 45 seconds.
- Every stage has a screenshot or evidence-file fallback.

## Context and framework content

- `AGENTS.md` is the metacognition/routing layer, not a giant prompt.
- The context spine holds current product, architecture, invariants, glossary, direction, and workflow.
- The planning trail holds intake, plan revisions, reviews, approval, execution review, evidence, and non-obvious notes.
- Skills and specialist agents are optional capability packs selected by the planner or executor; they do not change the two-loop lifecycle.

## Research wording

- Structured critique and debate can improve reasoning or oversight in controlled settings.
- Unstructured or long debate can drift and degrade.
- The framework is evidence-informed, not claimed as scientifically optimal.
- Graph engineering is an emerging label kept in repository reading notes, not the main talk.

## Visual direction
