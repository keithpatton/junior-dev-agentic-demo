# Product

Signal Desk is for a junior developer managing 5–20 active job applications. Its job is not merely to remember them; it should recommend the most defensible use of the next 30 minutes.

The primary outcome is immediate orientation: one concrete action, its company and role, and a plain-language reason. The pipeline supports that decision and lets the user record, stage, delete, and export opportunities.

This demo remains local and single-user. It excludes accounts, cloud sync, alerts, automatic job ingestion, and collaboration. The decisive walkthrough is a stage change that causes a credible recommendation change.
