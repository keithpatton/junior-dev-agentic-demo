# Product

Next Move helps a junior developer with 5–20 active job applications decide where to spend the next 30 minutes.

The primary screen must answer one question first: **What should I do next, and why?** The ledger is supporting context, not the product's centre. A recommendation is defensible when it is deterministic, names a concrete action, identifies the application, and explains the stage/deadline signals behind it.

Current scope is a local, single-user browser experience. It records applications, updates their stage, removes records, recommends one next action, and exports shareable application data. Authentication, team workflows, scraping, reminders, and cloud sync are deliberately outside the demo.

Success in the live walkthrough: change one application's stage and watch the recommended next move change for a reason the audience can understand.
